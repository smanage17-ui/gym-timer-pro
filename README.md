# Gym Timer Pro: Interval HIIT

A 100% free gym and interval timer for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription, no account.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6783197889?pt=129018807&ct=github&mt=8)**

## Timers

| Timer | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tabata | Work 5–90 s, rest 5–60 s, up to 20 rounds and 10 sets |
| EMOM | A new set every 15 s to 5 min, up to 30 rounds |
| AMRAP | 1–60 min time cap with tap-to-count rounds |
| Interval | Custom work and rest from 5 s to 5 min, up to 30 rounds |
| Round | Boxing and MMA style rounds |
| Lifting Pro | Warm-up, one-tap rest presets (30 s to 4 min), cool-down |
| Stretch | Timed stretch holds |

## Features

- Large full-screen ring you can read from across the gym
- Audio cues play over your music without pausing it
- Presets, workout history, day streak, monthly goal and calendar
- iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad; saves workouts to Apple Health
- Light, dark or system appearance
- English, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese (Brazil)

## Free means free

Every timer is included. There are no ads, no subscription, no account and no data collected.
